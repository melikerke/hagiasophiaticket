✦ About HagiaSophiaTicket

An independent guide to Hagia Sophia and nearby Sultanahmet.

HagiaSophiaTicket publishes practical visitor information for Hagia Sophia, Basilica Cistern, the Blue Mosque and the routes that connect these landmarks.

Editorial Responsibility

HagiaSophiaTicket is an independent visitor-information project edited by Melike, Founder & Editor. The site is not the official website of Hagia Sophia, Basilica Cistern or any ticket provider, and it does not operate the venues or set their rules and prices.

What We Cover

Hagia Sophia remains our main subject. We also cover nearby Sultanahmet landmarks when they help visitors build a realistic route, understand entry conditions or decide how much time to allow. This includes Basilica Cistern, the Blue Mosque and practical walking connections across the Old City.

How We Check Information

For venue rules, opening information and public prices, we look for the relevant venue or public-authority source. Ticket-provider pages are used for provider-specific inclusions, availability and final checkout prices. Time-sensitive details are dated, and key guides include source notes where readers can verify the information. Our full method is set out in the editorial policy.

How We Help Visitors

Our guides explain ticket inclusions, clothing requirements, timing, visit length, accessibility considerations and same-day routes. We separate confirmed visitor information from planning suggestions and avoid presenting changing rules as permanent facts.

Affiliate Transparency

Some links lead to third-party booking partners and are marked as affiliate links. If a reader books through one of them, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to the reader. That relationship does not make the partner an official venue source, and it does not determine the factual guidance we publish.

Corrections

If you find information that is outdated, unclear or inconsistent with a source, please use the contact page and include the page URL. We review corrections against the most relevant current source and update the guide when a change is warranted.
