This guided tour is one way to see three named Sultanahmet landmarks in one route: Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and the Basilica Cistern.

Quick answer

Choose this activity if you want a guide-led route through the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and Basilica Cistern. Confirm the selected language, group size, admission tickets and meeting point before payment.

Why this route works

Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque face the same historic square, while Basilica Cistern is only a short walk away. A guided route removes the guesswork around order, timing and what to notice inside each place.
