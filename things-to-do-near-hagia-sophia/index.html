Things To Do Near Hagia Sophia

⏱ 9 min read✦ Old City routeUpdated 2026

Hagia Sophia sits in the easiest sightseeing zone in Istanbul: Sultanahmet. Within a short walk, you can visit the Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern, Topkapi Palace, Sultanahmet Square and several guided Old City routes.

Quick answer

The best things to do near Hagia Sophia are Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern, Topkapi Palace, Sultanahmet Square, the Hagia Sophia upper-gallery visitor route and a guided Sultanahmet walking tour. Most first-time visitors should start with Hagia Sophia, then walk to the Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern and Topkapi Palace.

Best first-time route

Start above ground, then go underground, then finish with the palace

This order keeps the most crowded stops early and leaves the longer Topkapi Palace visit for the afternoon.

Local timing tipStart around 08:30-09:00 if Hagia Sophia is your priority. Avoid Friday midday around prayer access changes.

1. Visit Hagia Sophia Upper Gallery

For most first-time visitors, Hagia Sophia should come first. The paid tourist route uses the upper gallery, where you get elevated views of the interior, Byzantine mosaics and the building's scale from above.

The online ticket featured here starts from €28 and includes advance booking, mobile confirmation, audio commentary and AR features. It helps you avoid the ticket-office line, but mandatory security screening still applies.

2. Walk To The Blue Mosque

The Blue Mosque is directly across Sultanahmet Square. It is free to enter, but it is an active mosque, so visitor access pauses around prayer times and modest dress is required.

If you want a more structured visit, the Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque audio option starts from €30.49 and works well for travelers who prefer flexible timing instead of a live guided tour.

3. Go Underground At Basilica Cistern

Basilica Cistern is one of the easiest paid add-ons after Hagia Sophia. It is atmospheric, close and visually different from everything above ground.

The featured Basilica Cistern option is self-guided fast-track entry with a multilingual smartphone audio guide; no live guide is included. Download the audio before entering and bring headphones.

4. Add Topkapi Palace

Topkapi Palace is the biggest nearby attraction in terms of time. It is close to Hagia Sophia, but it deserves a slower visit because the courtyards, collections and Harem can easily take two to three hours.

The reviewed Istanbul Welcome Card ticket provides hosted entry followed by independent smartphone audio. Harem admission is excluded. Read the updated Topkapi ticket and entrance guide before choosing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do near Hagia Sophia?

The best things to do near Hagia Sophia are the Blue Mosque, Basilica Cistern, Topkapi Palace, Sultanahmet Square and the Hagia Sophia upper-gallery visitor route.

Can you visit Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque and Basilica Cistern in one day?

Yes. They are close enough to visit in one day, especially if you start early and avoid prayer-time access changes.

Is Topkapi Palace near Hagia Sophia?

Yes. Topkapi Palace is within walking distance of Hagia Sophia and is the best longer add-on for an Old City history day.

What ticket should first-time visitors book?

Most first-time visitors should choose either the Hagia Sophia online ticket, a Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque audio option, or a guided Old City route if they want multiple landmarks explained.

Is the Blue Mosque free?

Yes. The Blue Mosque is free to enter, but visitor access is managed around prayer times and modest dress is required.
