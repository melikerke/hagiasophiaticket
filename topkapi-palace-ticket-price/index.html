Topkapi Palace Ticket Price 2026

Official price: 2750 TLOnline price: check at checkout

Topkapi Palace pricing can be confusing because visitors compare the Turkish lira gate price with separate online self-guided and live-guided activities whose prices and option details can change.

Quick answer

The official National Palaces visitor information lists the foreign visitor combined ticket for Topkapi Palace + Hagia Irene + Harem as 2750 TL. The featured t1231414 online activity is self-guided; check its current price and confirm Harem, audio-guide, ticket-delivery and meeting details for the selected option. A separate t127010 live-guided activity includes Topkapi Palace and Harem tickets and offers English or German guide options.

Why does the online ticket cost more?

The online self-guided activity is not simply the raw gate tariff converted into euros. It adds advance-booking convenience, but Harem access, audio-guide availability and languages, ticket delivery, and any host meeting depend on the selected option and must be verified at checkout.

Does the Harem cost extra?

The official combined ticket includes the Harem. For the t1231414 self-guided activity, confirm Harem access for the selected option before payment. The separate t127010 live-guided activity includes both Topkapi Palace and Harem entry tickets.

Which option should you choose?

Choose the self-guided activity if you want to explore independently and are comfortable confirming the selected option's inclusions. Choose the separate Topkapi Palace and Harem guided tour if you want live explanation, both entry tickets and an English or German language option.

Starting times and planning

Sample starting times shown for Wednesday, July 22, 2026 include 9:30 AM, 12:00 PM and afternoon options. Availability changes by date, so check the booking page before building your itinerary.

Local planning tip

Do not leave Topkapi Palace too late in the day. It is much larger than Basilica Cistern or Blue Mosque, and the Harem adds extra walking and viewing time. A late-morning or early-afternoon slot is usually easier than rushing at the end of the day.
