One Day Sultanahmet Itinerary: Hagia Sophia, Topkapi & Cistern

6 min readUpdated September 2026

A good Sultanahmet day depends more on timing than distance: the main landmarks are close, but queues and prayer periods change the pace.

Quick answer

Start at Hagia Sophia at seasonal opening, visit Topkapi Palace before lunch, see Basilica Cistern in the afternoon and finish with the Blue Mosque and square.

Build a route for your time and visit date →

08:00 or 09:00 — Hagia Sophia

Arrive at summer or winter opening. Allow 60-90 minutes inside plus security and exterior photos.

10:00 — Topkapi Palace

Walk to the palace and use your hosted entry slot. Allow two to three hours for courtyards and main museum sections; the Harem needs separate access when available.

14:00 — Basilica Cistern

After lunch, walk to the cistern for a 45-60 minute visit. Its darker interior is comfortable during the hottest part of the day.

16:00 — Blue Mosque and square

Finish with the Blue Mosque outside prayer time, then photograph both monuments from Sultanahmet Square. Keep Friday prayer closures in mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can all four sites fit in one day?

Yes with an early start and realistic breaks.

Which attraction should come first?

Hagia Sophia.

How much walking is involved?

The sites are close, but expect a full day on your feet.

Is Friday ideal?

Another day is easier because prayer affects access.
