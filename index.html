Quick answers

Hagia Sophia ticket FAQ

Six quick answers before checkout.

See ticket conditions
Is this the official Hagia Sophia ticket website?

No. HagiaSophiaTicket is an independent visitor guide. Booking links open the selected product on Istanbul Welcome Card or GetYourGuide. Some are affiliate links.

Do tourists need a ticket for Hagia Sophia?

Yes. Cultural visitors use the paid upper-gallery route. The ground-floor prayer area is reserved for worship.

Can I buy Hagia Sophia tickets online?

Yes. Our featured Istanbul Welcome Card option sends an entry QR by email after booking, with no museum collection stop. Prices start from €28.45. Check your visit date, current price and final booking terms.

Does a skip-the-line ticket skip security?

No. Ticket-office arrangements depend on the selected option. Every visitor must still pass mandatory security screening.

How do I receive the main online ticket?

Istanbul Welcome Card emails the entrance QR and audio-guide instructions after booking. Save the QR and go to Hagia Sophia’s visitor entrance. The separate €28 kiosk-based alternative needs collection at the History & Experience Museum first.

What should I wear to Hagia Sophia?

Wear clothing that covers shoulders and knees. Women should also cover their hair because Hagia Sophia is an active mosque.
