Hagia Sophia ticket FAQ

Still deciding? These are the questions that matter most before checkout and arrival.

Check your date ↗
Is this the official Hagia Sophia ticket website?

No. HagiaSophiaTicket is an independent visitor guide. Online booking links open the exact activity on GetYourGuide, and some are affiliate links.

Do tourists need a ticket for Hagia Sophia?

Yes. Cultural visitors use the paid upper-gallery route. The ground-floor prayer area is reserved for worship.

Can I buy Hagia Sophia tickets online?

Yes. The main online option can be booked in advance through GetYourGuide. Check the selected date, cancellation policy and delivery instructions before payment.

Does a skip-the-line ticket skip security?

No. It avoids the ticket-office line, but every visitor must still pass mandatory security screening.

How do I receive the main online ticket?

For the main offer shown here, a code is sent on the visit day. Show it at the Hagia Sophia History & Experience Museum kiosk to collect the mosque ticket, then continue to security.

What should I wear to Hagia Sophia?

Wear clothing that covers shoulders and knees. Women should also cover their hair because Hagia Sophia is an active mosque.
