Best time to visit

Morning is usually the safest choice if you want fewer people. If you are visiting Hagia Sophia first, the Basilica Cistern also works well after lunch because the underground space is cooler and does not depend on daylight.

How long does it take?

Allow 30 to 60 minutes for most visits. If you use the self-guided smartphone audio, do not rush: the commentary helps explain why this underground water chamber mattered to Byzantine Constantinople.

Same-day route with Hagia Sophia

When both attractions are open, start with Hagia Sophia, then continue to Basilica Cistern while you are already in Sultanahmet. Compare Hagia Sophia + Basilica Cistern ticket options if you want to keep the route together.

A simple route is Hagia Sophia first, coffee or lunch around Sultanahmet, then Basilica Cistern. This avoids trying to do two ticketed interiors back to back while hungry or overheated.
