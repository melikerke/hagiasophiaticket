Basilica Cistern Opening Hours 2026

Daily 09:00–22:00Night Shift 19:30–22:00Sources reviewed 24 August 2026

This is an independent summary of published visitor information, not the Basilica Cistern's official website. The venue website currently lists daily opening from 09:00 to 22:00, while the cited 2026 ticket announcement separates daytime admission from the separately priced Night Shift.

Quick answer

Daily opening: 09:00–22:00. Daytime ticket window: 09:00–18:30. Night Shift: 19:30–22:00. Check the official website on the day of your visit because special events and maintenance can change access.

The attraction's overall closing time is 22:00, but admission conditions can differ between the daytime and Night Shift products. Do not assume a daytime ticket remains valid for the evening program.

Best Time To Visit

Early morning: the easiest default if you want to start before the Old City fills up. After Hagia Sophia: late morning or afternoon creates a natural two-stop route. Night Shift: best if the illuminated underground atmosphere is a priority and you accept the higher ticket price.

Crowd levels vary by season, weekends and tour schedules, so no time can guarantee an empty cistern.

What Is Night Shift?

Night Shift is the separately priced evening visit from 19:30 to 22:00. The announced 2026 foreign visitor price is 3,000 TL, compared with 1,950 TL during daytime hours.

Read the full Basilica Cistern evening and Night Shift guide for atmosphere, photography and day-versus-night advice.

How Long Does Basilica Cistern Take?

Allow 30 to 60 minutes inside. Add time for entry, the short walk from Hagia Sophia and photographs at the Medusa heads. A smartphone audio guide can make the route longer because it adds historical context.

Same-Day Route From Hagia Sophia

Visit Hagia Sophia first, then follow the 5–7 minute walk to Basilica Cistern. This gives you a clear contrast between the monumental upper gallery and the underground water chamber.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Basilica Cistern open every day?

Yes. The official website currently lists daily opening from 09:00 to 22:00.

What is the daytime ticket window?

The official 2026 announcement lists 09:00 to 18:30.

What time is Night Shift?

Night Shift is listed from 19:30 to 22:00 and uses a separately priced ticket.

How long should I spend inside?

Most visitors spend 30 to 60 minutes.

Should I visit before or after Hagia Sophia?

Most first-time visitors find Hagia Sophia first and Basilica Cistern afterward to be the simplest order.
