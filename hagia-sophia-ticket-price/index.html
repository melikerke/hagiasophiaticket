Hagia Sophia Ticket Price 2026

4 min readUpdated 9 September 2026
By Melike · Updated 9 September 2026

Published gallery admission: €25

DEM Museums’ visitor-area FAQ lists €25 admission and free entry for children under eight with ID. Source checked 9 September 2026.

First check which attraction the ticket covers

The mosque’s visitor gallery and the Hagia Sophia History and Experience Museum are different attractions. A museum ticket alone should not be treated as gallery admission. For a combined product, check that both places are named in the selected option.

Match the attraction name on the ticket to the place you intend to visit. Similar names or prices do not mean that two tickets include the same access.

Check ticket collection as well as price

The €28 and €28.45 options are both bought online. The difference is how you receive the entrance ticket. The €28 kiosk-based option requires a visit-day code exchange at the History & Experience Museum first. The €28.45 email-QR option sends the entrance QR by email after booking, so you can go directly to Hagia Sophia’s visitor entrance.

At these starting prices, €0.45 more removes the collection stop. The displayed starting price was €28.45 when checked on 9 September 2026. Check your visit date, current price and final total before payment. Compare equivalent entry and delivery arrangements.

The email-QR listing includes a 10-language audio app and free cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival. Download your audio before visiting and bring headphones. Security screening remains mandatory for both tickets.

Use the entrance and visitor-route guide and opening-hours guide when planning your arrival. A description of ticket-line access does not mean security can be skipped.

Choose the services you need

If you prefer an independent visit, compare entry-only admission first. If you want recorded commentary or a live guide, check the format and language. If you are adding another attraction, compare its individual admission with the combined total.

Common ticket questions

How much is Hagia Sophia gallery admission?

DEM Museums publishes a €25 visitor-area admission price. This is the gallery tariff, not a quote for every online package.

Is the History and Experience Museum included?

It is a separate attraction. Check that your selected ticket explicitly includes the mosque gallery, the History and Experience Museum, or both.

Are children free?

DEM Museums lists free visitor-area admission for children under eight with proof of age.

Does an online ticket skip security?

No. Ticket collection and ticket-office arrangements depend on the product. Mandatory security checks still apply.
