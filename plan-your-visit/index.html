02 / Arrive at the right place

Your collection point is not your entrance.

For the main ticket offer, start at the History & Experience Museum kiosk. Then walk to Hagia Sophia’s signed visitor-gallery entrance.

  1. 01
    Ticket collection

    History & Experience Museum

    For the main entry offer, exchange your visit-day code at this museum’s kiosk. It is a separate building on At Meydanı, near the Hippodrome. Follow your supplier’s instructions; other offers can use a different process.

    Find History & Experience Museum on Google Maps ↗
  2. 02
    Visitor entrance

    Hagia Sophia upper gallery

    At Hagia Sophia, follow the paid visitor-gallery signs and staff directions. The Ministry’s route description uses the south-side ramp and references the Ahmed III Fountain area. Temporary works may change the approach.

    Find Hagia Sophia upper gallery on Google Maps ↗
  3. 03
    Free mosque visit

    Blue Mosque

    Across Sultanahmet Square. Follow the visitor entrance signs; tourist entry pauses for prayer. Friday visitor entry begins at 14:30.

    Find Blue Mosque on Google Maps ↗
  4. 04
    Underground visit

    Basilica Cistern

    On Yerebatan Caddesi, a short walk from Hagia Sophia. Use the session on your ticket; the evening visit has separate admission arrangements.

    Find Basilica Cistern on Google Maps ↗
  5. 05
    Palace visit

    Topkapi Palace

    Beyond the Ahmed III Fountain area. Allow extra walking time through the palace grounds. Hosted tickets may require meeting elsewhere first; check the voucher. Closed Tuesdays.

    Find Topkapi Palace on Google Maps ↗

Before you set off

  • Times are estimates; queues and temporary closures can change the plan.
  • Hagia Sophia’s published Friday closure is 12:30–14:30. Blue Mosque visitor entry begins at 14:30 on Fridays.
  • Topkapi Palace is closed Tuesdays. Check the current hours before visiting.
  • Basilica Cistern’s day and evening sessions have separate admission arrangements.
