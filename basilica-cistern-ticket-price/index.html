Basilica Cistern Ticket Price 2026: Venue vs Online

Published venue tariffOptional audio and guided visitsUpdated 9 September 2026
By Melike · Independent visitor guide · Updated 9 September 2026

The first choice is daytime or Night Shift; the second is where to buy. This page compares the venue tariff with optional third-party products so you can check the same visit date, session and inclusions before paying.

Published admission prices

For foreign visitors, the announced tariff is 1,950 TL by day or 3,000 TL for Night Shift. These are venue admission prices, not a quote from an online reseller. TÜRSAB tariff announcement, 3 February 2026.

The announcement also lists free admission for children under seven visiting with their families. Eligibility depends on the visitor category, so do not apply the foreign-adult price to every member of your group.

The evening difference is 1,050 TL per foreign adult: for two adults that is an additional 2,100 TL before optional extras. Decide whether an evening visit matters enough to your itinerary before comparing reseller packages.

Audio is also available through the venue. The operator lists a daytime “Ticket + Audio Guide” option at 2,150 TL and audio separately at 300 TL. Confirm the language and conditions directly; a reseller is not required simply to obtain recorded commentary. Kültür AŞ visitor information.

Five checks before paying online

  1. Compare the final total in one currency. A TL admission price and a euro package price are not directly comparable. Include booking fees and your card’s conversion cost.
  2. Select the actual date and session. An attractive starting price may belong to another day, category or option.
  3. Read the entry instructions. Check whether you receive an entry ticket, a voucher to exchange, or a meeting-point instruction.
  4. Check what “fast-track” covers. It should describe a specific ticketing arrangement. Do not interpret it as a promise of no waiting or exemption from venue checks.
  5. Read cancellation and rescheduling terms. Save the confirmation and provider contact details before travelling.

The operator says payments at the museum entrance are accepted by credit/debit card and Istanbulkart. Prepare an accepted payment method if you plan to buy there.

Questions before you book

How much is Basilica Cistern entry in 2026?

The published foreign-visitor tariff is 1,950 TL for daytime admission and 3,000 TL for Night Shift. Check the venue’s tariff before buying.

Do I need to buy through a reseller?

No. Compare venue-issued admission first. A third-party product is optional; choose it only if its confirmed inclusions and booking conditions suit your visit.

Does an audio guide mean a live guided tour?

No. An audio guide is recorded commentary. For a live guide, the selected product must explicitly include a person leading the visit.

Will a daytime ticket cover Night Shift?

Do not assume so. Daytime and Night Shift have separate published tariffs; check the session on the ticket you select.
