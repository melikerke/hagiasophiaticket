Hagia Sophia visitor route: ticket, security, ramp, upper gallery

Hagia Sophia visitor route step by step

For the recommended email-QR ticket, save your entry QR before arrival, go to the signed visitor entrance, pass mandatory security and follow staff instructions to the visitor ramp and upper gallery. The route includes mosaics and interior views before the exit.

Museum kiosk exchange applies to the separate €28 collection-based option, not to every online ticket. With that option, exchange the visit-day code at the History & Experience Museum first. Both options still require security screening.

See the email-QR ticket from €28.45 or compare it with the €28 kiosk alternative.

Finding the tourist entrance

Approach from Sultanahmet Square and follow signs for the visitor experience or upper gallery. Entry arrangements can move during restoration or religious events, so use on-site signs rather than an old map screenshot.

What the route includes

The tourist route climbs to the upper gallery for elevated views, Byzantine mosaics and perspectives across the prayer hall. Uneven historic surfaces make comfortable shoes useful.

Ground floor and worship

The ground floor follows mosque access rules and is primarily reserved for prayer. A free worship entrance is not the same as the paid cultural visitor route.

Arrival checklist

Keep your actual entry QR and any required ID ready, download your audio, dress modestly and check Friday visiting hours. Follow your booking’s delivery instructions: only kiosk-based tickets require the museum collection stop. Security screening remains mandatory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do tourists enter?

Follow the signed upper-gallery visitor entrance near Sultanahmet Square.

Can tourists use the ground floor?

The ground floor is primarily reserved for worship.

Is security required?

Yes for every visitor.

Is the route accessible?

Historic ramps and steps can be difficult; check current assistance before arrival.

What is the Hagia Sophia visitor route?

Have your valid entry ticket ready, pass mandatory security, then follow the visitor ramp to the upper gallery, mosaics and exit. Collection or host meeting requirements depend on the ticket; the recommended emailed entry QR needs no museum pickup.
