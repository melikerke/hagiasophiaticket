The Basilica Cistern is one of the easiest and most rewarding add-ons after Hagia Sophia: it is underground, atmospheric, close to Sultanahmet Square and usually takes less than an hour.

Quick answer

The self-guided Basilica Cistern fast-track ticket featured here starts from €57.47 and includes multilingual smartphone audio guidance. The official daytime foreign visitor ticket is listed at 1,950 TL, so the online fast-track audio ticket costs more because it adds smartphone audio context and fast-track convenience.

Best use case

Book this option if you want Basilica Cistern to be more than a quick photo stop. If you are mainly comparing costs, read the Basilica Cistern ticket price guide. If you are planning the day around Hagia Sophia, check the opening hours and short walking route.

Official price vs fast-track audio ticket price

The official Basilica Cistern daytime foreign visitor ticket is listed at 1,950 TL. The self-guided fast-track audio ticket offered here starts from €57.47. That difference is important to explain clearly: the fast-track audio ticket is not the cheapest possible entry, but it adds multilingual smartphone audio and fast-track ticket-line convenience.

Opening hours

The official visitor information lists the Basilica Cistern as open every day from 09:00 to 22:00. The regular daytime ticket window is listed as 09:00-18:30, with a separate Night Shift ticket from 19:30-22:00.

What you will see inside

Inside the Basilica Cistern, look for the long rows of columns, reflected water, changing light and the famous Medusa heads. The atmosphere is very different from Hagia Sophia: darker, cooler and much more cinematic.

Best route from Hagia Sophia

Walk from Hagia Sophia toward Sultanahmet Square and follow signs toward Yerebatan Caddesi. The walk usually takes only a few minutes, which is why Hagia Sophia + Basilica Cistern is one of the strongest same-day combinations in the Old City.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Basilica Cistern worth visiting?

Yes, especially if you are already visiting Hagia Sophia. It is close, visually unique and takes less time than most major museums.

Is this the cheapest Basilica Cistern ticket?

No. It is a self-guided fast-track audio ticket. The official daytime foreign visitor ticket is listed at 1,950 TL, while this fast-track audio ticket starts from €57.47.

How long should I spend inside?

Most visitors spend 30 to 60 minutes.

Can I combine Basilica Cistern with Hagia Sophia?

Yes. They are only a short walk apart and work very well in the same Sultanahmet route.
