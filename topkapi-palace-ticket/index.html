Dates, times and fulfillment

Availability changes by date. Confirm the current price, selected entry time, ticket delivery and any meeting instructions on the booking page before building your itinerary.

Official gate price vs online option

The official National Palaces visitor page lists a foreign visitor combined ticket for Topkapi Palace + Hagia Irene + Harem. The online activity featured here is a separate self-guided product, so do not assume it has the same inclusions: compare the current price and selected-option details before payment.

Best order with Hagia Sophia

Visit Hagia Sophia early, then continue to Topkapi Palace before lunch or early afternoon. Topkapi takes longer than most Sultanahmet attractions, so give yourself at least 2 to 3 hours; allow additional time if your selected option includes the Harem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the self-guided activity include the Harem?

Harem access depends on the selected option; confirm it before payment.

Does it include an audio guide or host meeting?

Confirm audio-guide availability and languages, ticket delivery, and any host meeting instructions for the selected option at checkout.

Is this a guided tour?

No. The featured t1231414 activity is self-guided. The separate t127010 activity is a live-guided Topkapi and Harem tour with both tickets included and English or German options.

Is Topkapi Palace closed on Tuesday?

The official National Palaces page lists Topkapi Palace as closed on Tuesdays.

Can I combine Topkapi Palace with Hagia Sophia?

Yes. They are close enough for the same day, but start early because Topkapi Palace needs more time.
